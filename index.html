Frequently Asked

Will a tracker lower my van insurance?

Most insurers offer reduced premiums when a Thatcham-approved tracker is professionally fitted. We provide the installation certificate you'll need.

Can the dash cam record while the van is parked?

Yes — our recommended units include 24/7 parking mode with motion and impact detection, hard-wired with a battery cut-off to protect your starter battery.

Where do you install?

We're a mobile-fitting team covering London and the wider South East. We come to your home, yard or depot — most installs are completed in a single visit.

Is the footage admissible as evidence?

Yes. Footage is timestamped, GPS-stamped and stored on both microSD and cloud — accepted by police, insurers and the courts.

Do you fit on fleets?

Absolutely. We work with fleets from 3 to 300+ vehicles, with consolidated reporting, driver ID and a single point of contact.
